Problem & goal
🎨

The problem

Art learners switch between multiple sites for references, tutorials, and buying supplies. No single place serves all three needs with a clean, calm experience.

✦

The goal

Design an all-in-one store where artists can explore references, learn techniques, and shop for handmade goods — all with a cohesive visual identity.

Design decisions

Soft purple palette

Purple reads as creative and gender-neutral — perfect for an art audience. Avoids the harsh contrast of black/white-only stores.

3-section navigation

Split into Shop, Tutorials, and References so users immediately know what the site offers without reading any copy.

Mock API for products

Product data is fetched dynamically — simulates a real e-commerce backend and makes the store easy to expand.

Integrated crochet business

Ivy & Stardust products live inside the store — merging the personal small business with the platform naturally.
